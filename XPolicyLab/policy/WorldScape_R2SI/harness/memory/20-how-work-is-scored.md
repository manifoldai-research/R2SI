## How work is scored

Jobs are usually judged as follows; the instruction decides where it says otherwise. The scene is read at the end of every native step, and strictly. What must be true together has to be true at the same step; nothing has to be held longer than that, and what happens only between steps is not seen.
- Do what the instruction asks, exactly, including how it says to do it: every named object in the asked relation, as exactly as you can measure, and within millimetres where parts fit together. Expect orientation to count even when it is not mentioned: what stands must stand upright, what is built or set out together should be lined up, and an object put on a marked or given pose must match that pose too. Check each item by measuring the object again, not the hand, before you consider it done. Where the instruction states an amount, go clearly past it on the side it allows, and where it only says to pick or lift something, raise it high and clear of everything around, since a held object can sag or slip. Fix what is off while you still have steps.
- Inside means entirely inside, below the rim and not leaning over an edge. A part stuck high or only partly inserted is not in place, so measure its height after you let go.
- A stated order is part of the job: do the steps in that order and let each one show before the next. When the instruction gives a count, do exactly that many and keep your own tally.
- Leave alone what you were not asked to move: something knocked over or shifted counts against you.
- Work counts as items or groups of items are finished and is kept once counted, but it is read only at a step where both grippers are fully open. A hand still holding something, or only partly open, blocks everything, including what the other hand has finished. So finish and release one item before starting the next, and after each item open fully.
- Unless told otherwise, the whole job ends with everything released, the grippers open and both arms back at their initial poses: release, withdraw, return. robo.state() gives home_steps; include the return motion in your plan. Returning earns nothing by itself; it counts only once everything is in place. Finish and release items promptly, then return after the requested work is in place.
- Let things settle before you judge them, and measure again after any later change.
